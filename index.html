Monthly climate — Fording River

Station: Environment Canada Fording River Cominco (climate ID 1152899, 1,585 m), daily records 1970–2007. Blank = station median (hover a box to see its P10 / P90). An edited value moves the whole P10–P90 range with it. Temperatures are corrected from the station to the haul-road elevation (−0.6 °C per 100 m). Heavy-snow days = snow days that are part of a storm totalling ≥ 5 cm (consecutive snow days added up). Dense-fog days: no Elk Valley station records visibility; the default is Cranbrook airport (940 m, days with ≥ 3 h below 1 km visibility), which likely under-states mountain fog — please replace it with your site's experience.

S Seasonal schedule (month-by-month weather)

Each month's days are split into dense fog, heavy snowfall, light snowfall, rain, dry with a snow-packed road, and dry days, from the site climate. Every day type is run through the same fleet, road and shift model, then weighted by its number of days. The weather slider in the top bar moves every month from the station median towards a 1-in-10 good year (left) or a 1-in-10 bad year (right). Working days per month = calendar days × working days per year ÷ 365.

6 Operations & Method

Each loss is counted once: meetings come off the shift; availability (machine condition) covers breakdowns; standby covers the listed waits; job efficiency covers only small operational delays; weather, dust and darkness are a separate condition factor.
